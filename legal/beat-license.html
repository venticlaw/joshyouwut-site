Beat License Terms

These public terms explain the standard JoshYouWut beat-license paths. Your order confirmation, invoice, checkout record, or signed agreement controls the exact beat, tier, price, buyer, and final rights.

Starter Lease

  • Public starting price: $29.
  • Non-exclusive use for one song release.
  • MP3 plus WAV if listed in the order.
  • No stems.
  • Up to $2,500 gross revenue and 100,000 combined streams/downloads/views unless the order says otherwise.
  • One official video or visualizer on artist-controlled channels.
  • No paid ads, SYNC, or Content ID unless approved in writing.

Premium Lease

  • Public starting price: $79.
  • Non-exclusive use for one serious release.
  • MP3, WAV, and available tracked stems/trackouts.
  • Up to $10,000 gross revenue and 500,000 combined streams/downloads/views unless the order says otherwise.
  • Official video, visualizer, short-form clips, and artist-controlled social content allowed.
  • Paid artist-promo ads allowed up to $1,000 total ad spend unless the order says otherwise.
  • No SYNC or Content ID unless approved in writing.

Exclusive Buyout

  • Custom quotes start at $500+.
  • Exclusivity begins only after full payment clears and written terms are confirmed.
  • Existing valid non-exclusive licenses sold before exclusivity remain valid unless separately bought out.
  • MP3, WAV, and available tracked stems/trackouts are included.
  • Exclusive use rights do not automatically transfer copyright ownership unless the agreement says so.
  • Publishing, songwriting, master ownership, royalty splits, credits, PRO registration, and Content ID rules must be stated in writing.

Credit

Default public credit: Produced by JoshYouWut. Acceptable short credit: Beat by JoshYouWut.

Restrictions

Buyers may not resell, sublicense, lease, distribute, or give away a beat as a standalone instrumental; claim ownership of the beat itself; upload it as a sample pack, loop pack, stock asset, or AI-training dataset; or use it in unlawful, infringing, deceptive, or high-risk brand contexts without written approval.
