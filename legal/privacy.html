Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Joshua "JoshYouWut" Walker, operating as JoshYouWut, may collect, use, and protect information submitted through the website, forms, product pages, and buyer inquiry surfaces.

Information Collected

JoshYouWut may collect first name, email address, phone number, artist or company name, social profiles, music links, selected beat, selected package, selected product, timeline, budget range, release goals, rights needs, support messages, and submission metadata such as page URL and timestamp.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, scope beat licenses, quote artist packages, manage plugin interest, prepare invoices or order confirmations, provide support, maintain buyer history, improve offers, and meet legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or security obligations.

Product Lists And Marketing

If you join a product list, JoshYouWut may use your submitted information to send compatibility notes, availability updates, purchase-path updates, and related product information. You can request removal by emailing support.

Submitted Music And Project Materials

Demos, links, stems, lyrics, references, and project materials are used to evaluate inquiries, scope projects, prepare quotes, provide services, and provide support. Do not submit materials you are not authorized to share.

Sharing

JoshYouWut does not sell buyer contact information. Information may be shared with service providers or collaborators only as needed for hosting, forms, email, payment, delivery, accounting, customer support, production, mixing/mastering, legal review, or fraud prevention.

Payments

When live payments are enabled, payments should be processed by an approved third-party payment provider. JoshYouWut should not store raw card numbers or payment credentials in site files, public repos, emails, or unencrypted documents.

Storage And Retention

Lead, order, support, delivery, tax, and accounting information may be retained as long as needed for business, support, accounting, legal, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution purposes.

Your Choices

You may request correction or deletion of contact information by emailing support@joshyouwut.com. Some records may need to be retained for legal, tax, accounting, dispute, fraud-prevention, or order-history reasons.

Security

No website, email, or online service is perfectly secure. Do not submit passwords, private keys, payment card numbers, sensitive government identifiers, or other secrets through ordinary inquiry forms.
