# JoshYouWut Brand Standards

Open Design source pass: web prototype, pricing page, social carousel, X/social post card, and HyperFrames social overlay conventions.

## Brand Position
JoshYouWut is a producer-led music commerce brand for serious artists, managers, creators, and brands. The brand sells a stronger record path: beats, custom production, mixing/mastering packages, SYNC-ready cues, and producer-built tools.

## Core Message
Do not buy a beat. Build the record.

## Visual System
- Primary field: black / coal.
- Primary type: paper / white.
- Conversion accent: JYW orange-gold.
- Secondary accents: coral for urgency, mint for confirmation, JYW blue for technical/plugin or data moments.
- Texture: waveform rhythm, studio-console structure, hard section bands, crisp type.
- Avoid: owner notes, backend copy, generic music stock visuals, decorative blobs, vague "beats for everyone" claims.

## Type Direction
- Headlines: uppercase, heavy, compact, short lines.
- Body: practical and buyer-facing.
- Metadata: uppercase labels with moderate tracking.
- Social captions: one idea per asset, no long explanations.

## Logo Rules
- Use `assets/jyw-logo.svg`.
- Keep the logo on black, coal, or high-contrast neutral fields.
- Do not recolor, stretch, crop, or bury it in busy imagery.

## Social Kit
### YouTube Banner
Use `2560x1440`. Put important content inside the center `1546x423` safe area. Lead with brand, offer, and the three money paths: artist packages, beat licensing, Your Mix Sucks.

### Instagram Carousel
Use `1080x1080` slides. Default rhythm: hook, offer, action. Each slide needs a short headline, useful metadata, and a visible brand mark.

### Threads Post
Write direct text first, then support it with a share card. The post should be useful even if the image does not load.

### YouTube Beat Video
Use a still `1920x1080` image with the beat title, BPM, key, lane, and a licensing CTA. Audio comes from an existing beat preview.
