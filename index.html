Your Mix Sucks

The brutally useful mix coach for Logic Pro. Drop it on a track, bus, or mix bus, hit play, and get a 0-100 mix read plus plain-English feedback on frequency balance, dynamics, stereo field, gain staging, masking, and what to fix next. Mix Notes now preserve an Overall tip plus timestamped Timeline notes inside the Logic project.

  • Analysis-only: it listens, scores, and teaches without changing your audio.
  • Timestamped Mix Notes append as the track plays and restore when the Logic project reopens.
  • Logic verification confirmed playback analysis, saved timeline notes, compact GAIN label, and FFT edge labels.

BassPhat

BassPhat is the Josh Labs low-end tool for producers who want bass weight without losing control. It focuses on harmonic color, sub discipline, mono-safe low end, and output matching so the bottom feels bigger while the mix still translates.

  • Simple booster/color workflow for bass, 808s, kicks, and low-end groups.
  • Designed for EDM and rap sessions where the bottom has to feel physical without wrecking headroom.
  • Pairs with Your Mix Sucks: diagnose the mix first, then shape the low end with intent.
